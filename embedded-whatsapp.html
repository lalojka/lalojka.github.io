How Coexistence Works

WhatsApp Coexistence allows the same phone number to operate from the mobile app and the API simultaneously.

📱

1. Connect your number

Link the same number you already use in WhatsApp Business app. Your chats and contacts are preserved.

💻

2. Reply from anywhere

Your agents use the CRM for templates and automation. You keep using the mobile app to reply quickly.

☁️

3. Stay in sync

Incoming messages arrive on both sides. Keep the app open at least once every 14 days to avoid deactivation.

Who is V4 API EPM for?

V4 API EPM is designed for SMBs and agencies already using WhatsApp Business app who want to scale without losing simplicity.

Typical case: a 3-person sales team. The owner replies from mobile while out in the field. Agents use the CRM to send catalogs, automated follow-ups, and assign chats. Everyone uses the same verified number.

  • Send approved templates from CRM without changing phone numbers
  • Quick replies from mobile app for the owner or manager
  • Unified history: one number, two access channels

Typical workflow

1 Customer sends a message to your WhatsApp number
2 CRM receives it and assigns to the available agent
3 Agent replies from CRM using an approved template
4 Owner follows and continues the conversation from mobile if needed

Important Limitations of Coexistence

Meta requires these limitations to be explicitly declared. These are expected platform behaviors.

✅ Works in Coexistence ❌ Does NOT work in Coexistence
1:1 messages, approved templates, voice notes Broadcast lists from the mobile app
WhatsApp Business catalog Disappearing messages
Calls from the mobile app Live location sharing
Chat history visible on both sides Group chats managed via API
⚠️ Green verified badge: The green verified badge is temporarily disabled during Coexistence. This is expected Meta platform behavior and does not indicate an issue with your account.
⚠️ 14-day rule: To keep Coexistence active, the WhatsApp Business app must be opened at least once every 14 days. Failure to do so will deactivate Coexistence mode.
