Frequently Asked Questions

Is EPM APP really free?

Yes! EPM APP is free for everyone, with all features included. No hidden costs.

Absolutely. We never store your Facebook password. All authentications are handled securely via OAuth by Facebook.

What metrics can I export?

You can export a wide range of metrics from Facebook Pages and Instagram Business accounts, including reach, engagement, followers, and more.

Can I manage multiple pages?

Yes! EPM APP lets you connect and manage as many Facebook and Instagram accounts as you wish.
